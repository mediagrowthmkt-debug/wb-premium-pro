Home Additions

One Contractor. The Whole Job.

Adding to your home is one of the biggest projects you'll ever do. We handle the whole job — engineering coordination, permits, foundation, framing, roofing, electrical, plumbing, drywall, and finish work. You won't be calling five different companies. You'll be calling one.

What's Included

  • Engineering coordination (when required)
  • Permit applications and inspections
  • Foundation and framing
  • Roofing and exterior work
  • Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC (with licensed subs)
  • Insulation, drywall, and finish carpentry
  • Paint and trim
  • Final walk-through

"Adding to a home is one of the most variable projects in residential construction. We give you a full, itemized estimate after an on-site walkthrough — so there are no guesses and no surprises."

Timeline: 8 to 16 weeks, depending on size.
